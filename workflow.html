Request Submission

The staff member completes and submits the Job Order/Requisition Form.

The system records the request and sends the request to the Coordinator.

Coordinator Review

The Coordinator checks whether the form is complete, valid, and necessary.

  • If rejected: The Coordinator records the reason, and the requester is notified by SMS or email.
  • If approved: The request is forwarded to Finance.

Finance Approval

The Administrative and Finance Officer reviews the request, inventory status, budget, or charge account.

  • If rejected: The reason is recorded, and the requester is notified by SMS or email.
  • If approved: The requester is notified, and the request proceeds to the Custodian.

Custodian

The Custodian verifies whether the requested items and quantities are available.

The availability status is recorded as:

  • Fully available
  • Partially available
  • Unavailable

Fulfillment

  • Fully available: The Custodian prepares and releases the items.
  • Partially available: The Custodian records the shortage and informs the requester. Unless the requester agrees to a partial release, the entire request is held until Purchasing obtains the remaining items.
  • Unavailable: The entire request is forwarded to Purchasing.

Procurement

Purchasing processes the required items.

Target turnaround time: Three business days, subject to supplier availability and applicable purchasing procedures.

The requester receives status updates by SMS or email.

Release and Completion

Once the items are available, the Custodian releases them to the requester.

The requester signs an acknowledgment of receipt.

The request is marked Completed.
